## 0. Global setup (the lead builds this first, as task P0)

### 0.1 Composition
- `index.html` contains:
  - one `#stage` of 1920×1080 with `overflow:hidden`, background `#000`;
  - one `<section class="scene" id="<id>">` per scene, each holding its `html/<id>.html` fragment;
  - the stage-level `<video>` clips (§0.5);
  - the global layers `#world` (bottom), `#phone` (the rig, §0.4), `#fx` (grain and vignette, top).
- **One paused GSAP timeline**, `window.__timelines["main"]`, 45.00 s long, built by `js/main.js`:
  ```js
  const tl = gsap.timeline({paused:true});
  buildGlobal(tl);
  for (const id of SCENES) (await import(`./scenes/${id}.js`)).build(tl, document.getElementById(id));
  ```
  The import order is the scene order. In practice the lead inlines plain `<script>` tags and each file calls
  `PP.scene(id, build)`. The required signature is **`build(tl, root)`**, and every tween is placed at **absolute seconds**.
- **Files per scene:**
  - `js/scenes/<id>.js`, which exports `build(tl, root)`;
  - `css/<id>.css`, with every selector prefixed by `#<id>`;
  - `html/<id>.html`, the markup fragment;
  - `tools/assemble.py`, which builds `index.html` from the fragments, `css/*.css` and the video table in §0.5.
- **Visibility:** a scene shows its root with `tl.set(root,{opacity:1},IN)` and hides it with `tl.set(root,{opacity:0},OUT)`. Never tween
  `visibility`, `display` or `autoAlpha` on a `<section>`.
- **Determinism:**
  - no `Math.random`, `Date`, timers, rAF or callbacks;
  - use `PP.rng(seed)` and `PP.drive()`;
  - write `fromTo(..., {immediateRender:false})` whenever a property is set more than once;
  - gate the build only on `document.fonts.load`, and preload images with `<link rel="preload">`;
  - no `will-change` (remove the one in `phone-screen.css`);
  - no `backdrop-filter`.
- **Lint:** `npx hyperframes lint` with 0 errors, and `npx hyperframes check`. Large per-glyph animations go through **one** `PP.drive` setter each,
  to avoid `timeline_track_too_dense`. Update `verify_output.sh` for 1350 frames / 45.00 s.

### 0.3 Timing sources
- **`js/cues.js`** holds the music cues. They are frozen and must never be edited:
  `HIT1=1.0, HIT9=9.0, ACC13=13.0, RISER0=21.0, DROP=25.0, STOP=40.5, LOGO=41.0, BEAT=0.5`.
- **`js/vo.js`** is generated by `tools/build_vo.py` and contains `window.VO = {L01:{at,end,text,words:[[t,"word"],…]},…}`.
  `lib.js` adds:
  ```js
  VO.w = (id, i) => typeof i === 'number' ? VO[id].words[i][0]
                                          : VO[id].words.find(w => w[1].toLowerCase().startsWith(i.toLowerCase()))[0];
  ```
  **Until the VO exists**, the lead ships a stub `js/vo.js` with each line at its BRIEF §6 target start, and word i at
  `at + 0.36·i`. Scenes must read `VO.w()` for every word-locked event and **never hard-code** a word time.
- Where a scene says "on `VO.w('L05',0)`", the tween starts at that time. "−6 f" means `VO.w(...) − 6/30`.
- **Clamp rule:** every word-locked event in a scene is clamped into that scene's window. The scene file
  `console.warn`s whenever a clamp fires.

**`#phone`** is a 1920×1080 group with `transform-origin:0 0`, transformed by `translate(tx,ty) scale(g)`. Its rest pose is g=1, tx=ty=0,
and it is pixel-identical to Blender `flyin` frame 89 (cutcheck 2.7 levels mean).

**Screen geometry** comes from `ROOT/assets/iphone/screen_rect.json` at "1920x1080":
- screen x **759.85**, y **105.27**, w 400.31, h 869.45, radius 51.9;

- the `.ps` root (402×874 pt) is placed at `left:759.85px; top:105.27px; transform:scale(0.99580)`, with
  `border-radius:52.12px` (= 51.9/K) and `overflow:hidden`.

**Point conversions:**
- Screen point to stage at the rest pose: `base(X,Y) = (759.85 + 0.9958·X, 105.27 + 0.9958·Y)`.
- With the camera: `stage(X,Y) = (tx + g·baseX, ty + g·baseY)`.
- Page point (px,py) at scroll s, to screen point: `(px, 62 + py − s)`. This is the `phone-screen.js` convention.
  The sticky nav occupies screen y 62–133 once s ≥ 46.
- The Safari bar covers screen y 796–846, with the URL pill at x 74–328. The home indicator is at y 861–866.

**API (all seek-safe):**
- `PH.cam(tl, t0, dur, {g, ox, oy, cx, cy}, ease)`: places screen point (ox,oy) at stage (cx,cy) at scale g, using
  `tx = cx − g·(759.85+0.9958·ox)` and `ty = cy − g·(105.27+0.9958·oy)`. It tweens g, tx and ty together.
- `PH.stage(X,Y,t)`: the stage point at time t. Use it to place stage-space callouts, FLIP origins and leader lines (drive them per frame).
- `PH.show(tl, t, page)`, `PH.scroll(tl, page, s0, s1, t0, dur, ease)` and `PH.flick(tl, page, s0, s1, t0)`. The flick is an 8 f `power2.in` to 20 % of the
  distance, then 14 f `expo.out`, with blur = min(2, 0.04·|Δs per frame|) pt on that page's `.ps-img`.
- `PH.tap(tl, t, X, Y)`: the `.ps-tap` ring (no finger). Fill `rgba(18,58,120,.22)`, ring 1.5 pt `rgba(18,58,120,.55)` (white `.45/.8` on navy
  buttons). Scale 0.4→1, opacity 0.6→0, 12 f `power2.out`.
- `PH.press(tl, t, page, rect)`: a sprite cropped from the same page PNG scales 0.97 over 3 f `power2.out`, then springs back over 6 f with `back.out(0.8)`.
  **State changes land at tap + 2 f.**
- `PH.push(tl, t0, fromPage, toPage, sTo)`: Safari push over 12 f, `cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1)`. The incoming page goes x 402→0 pt with a left-edge
  shadow; the outgoing page goes 0→−120 pt and dims 0→20 %.
- `PH.navBadge(tl, t, srcPage)`: swaps the stuck `.ps-nav` background to `srcPage`'s nav (for example `cart1` shows badge "1"), with a pop of 1→1.3→1 over 8 f
  `back.out(0.8)` on a 24×24 pt crop around (329,70) in screen points.
- `PH.barFill(tl, t0, dur, page, f0, f1, color0, color1)`: an HTML overlay over the captured ship bar. The track is `#E6EBF2` and the fill goes from fraction f0 to f1.
  Measure the bar rect on the clean captures (≈ page x 37–365, y 293–302). The fractions are measured from pixels, never computed:
  cart1 ≈ 0.41, cart2 ≈ 0.80, cart3 = 1.
- `PH.crop(page, rect)`: a div showing that page-point rect of the capture, in screen points. Use it for FLIP sources.
